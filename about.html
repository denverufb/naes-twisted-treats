MEET THE OWNER

Easter Nae Nae Robinson

Easter Nae Nae Robinson is a St. Louis-area entrepreneur, bartender, and the owner of Nae’s Twisted Treats, a Black-owned restaurant in Ferguson, Missouri.

She built Nae’s Twisted Treats from the ground up. Faith and determination carried her through the work of opening a restaurant, and she wants the business to create opportunities and lift up the Ferguson community.

Read Easter’s introduction on Facebook

Beyond the restaurant

Mobile bartending

Through Nae’s Twisted Drinks Mobile Bartending, Easter brings bar service to private parties and special events across the St. Louis area.

Custom desserts

Easter makes banana pudding, cheesecake, custom cakes, and gift baskets for holidays and special occasions.

Part of Ferguson

Local food creators have shared visits to Nae’s Twisted Treats, and the Ferguson Emerging Collective has spotlighted the restaurant as part of Ferguson’s small-business community.
